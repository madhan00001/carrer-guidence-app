# Career Profile: UI/UX Product Designer

## 1. Role Overview
A UI/UX Designer crafts intuitive, engaging, and accessible digital user interfaces. They conduct user research, produce wireframes and interactive prototypes, construct cohesive design systems, and collaborate with developers to bridge the gap between user intent and code execution.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Conduct user interviews, usability audits, and competitive benchmarking.
- Create information architectures, user flow diagrams, and low-to-high fidelity wireframes.
- Build interactive clickable prototypes in Figma.
- Maintain atomic design systems (color tokens, typography, component states).
- Test prototypes with real users and iterate based on quantitative/qualitative metrics.

## 3. Required Technical Skills & Tools
- **Design & Prototyping Tools:** Figma (Auto Layout, Components, Variants, Variables), Adobe XD, Framer.
- **Design Systems & Foundations:** Atomic design, typography hierarchies, contrast ratios (WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA), spacing scales.
- **User Research Methods:** User personas, customer journey maps, card sorting, A/B testing, heatmaps.
- **Front-End Familiarity:** HTML5, CSS3, Flexbox/Grid, Tailwind principles (to design with engineering constraints in mind).

## 4. Required Soft Skills
- Deep user empathy and psychological awareness of cognitive load.
- Storytelling and presenting design decisions rationally to business stakeholders.
- Receptivity to constructive design feedback and collaborative iteration.

## 5. Industry Certifications
- Google UX Design Professional Certificate (Coursera)
- Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) UX Certification
- Interaction Design Foundation (IxDF) Specialist Tracks

## 6. Recommended Learning Resources & Courses
- "Don't Make Me Think, Revisited" by Steve Krug
- "The Design of Everyday Things" by Don Norman
- "Refactoring UI" by Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger
- Figma Community Design Systems and tutorials

## 7. Recommended Portfolio Projects
1. **Student Academic & Career Roadmap App (Case Study):** End-to-end design from user interviews, wireframes, persona discovery to responsive mobile & desktop high-fidelity Figma prototype.
2. **Accessible E-Commerce Checkout Overhaul:** Redesign targeting cart abandonment reduction with accessibility audit and A/B test proposal.
3. **Multi-Brand Design System in Figma:** Comprehensive component library featuring tokens for dark/light modes, buttons, forms, and micro-interactions.

## 8. Interview Topics & Preparation Checklist
- Portfolio presentation: Walking through a case study from problem statement to final outcome with quantified metrics.
- Whiteboard design challenge: Rapidly solving an ambiguous product problem within 45 minutes.
- How you resolve design disagreements with engineering or product managers.
- Accessibility best practices and color blindness considerations.
