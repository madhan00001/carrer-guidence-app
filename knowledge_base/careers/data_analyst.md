# Career Profile: Data Analyst

## 1. Role Overview
A Data Analyst inspects, cleans, transforms, and models data to uncover actionable insights, identify market trends, and support strategic decision-making across business and operational functions.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
- Query and aggregate large relational datasets using SQL.
- Build interactive executive dashboards in tools like Power BI or Tableau.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and hypothesis testing.
- Translate ambiguous business questions into measurable analytical metrics.
- Present data narratives and actionable recommendations to stakeholders.

## 3. Required Technical Skills & Languages
- **Data Querying & Storage:** Advanced SQL (Window functions, CTEs, Joins, Aggregations, Indexing).
- **Programming & Scripting:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) or R.
- **Spreadsheets:** Advanced Microsoft Excel (XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, Power Query, VBA basics).
- **Business Intelligence (BI) Tools:** Microsoft Power BI (DAX, Power Query M), Tableau, Looker Studio.
- **Statistical Knowledge:** Descriptive statistics, probability distributions, A/B testing, correlation vs causation.

## 4. Required Soft Skills
- Storytelling with data: conveying complex numbers in simple business terms.
- Critical thinking and skepticism towards outlier/dirty data.
- Active listening and stakeholder interview skills.
- Time management across multiple reporting deliverables.

## 5. Industry Certifications
- Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate (Coursera)
- Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300)
- Tableau Certified Data Analyst
- IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate

## 6. Recommended Learning Resources & Courses
- "SQL for Data Science" (UC Davis on Coursera)
- "Data Analysis with Python" (FreeCodeCamp)
- "Storytelling with Data" by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic
- Maven Analytics BI and Power BI Mastery Tracks

## 7. Recommended Portfolio Projects
1. **E-Commerce Customer Retention & Churn Dashboard:** Power BI report analyzing cohort retention, revenue trends, and RFM customer segmentation.
2. **Healthcare Resource Utilization EDA:** Python Jupyter notebook performing exploratory data analysis on hospital admissions and seasonal trends.
3. **Retail Sales SQL Analysis Pipeline:** Complete SQL script suite calculating MoM growth, moving averages, and product affinity patterns.

## 8. Interview Topics & Preparation Checklist
- Live SQL query coding (Window functions like RANK, DENSE_RANK, LEAD, LAG).
- Business case study: "Our active users dropped 12% last week, how do you diagnose this?"
- Metric definitions (CAC, LTV, Retention Rate, Churn, ROI).
- Portfolio review: Explaining a dashboard you designed and the business decision it enabled.
